# Evidence terminology review

The catalog includes 31 source-linked terminology candidates for SOTIO and two for Michigan. Each row in `terminology.json` is keyed to an exact study, criterion and evidence label. Its note describes what the proposed code can establish. Verification against a terminology authority establishes code identity. The specific hospital field, value system, units, provenance and episode linkage still need testing against the feed. Executable adapter and synthetic fixture results are reported separately.

## SOTIO

The laboratory mappings distinguish the analyte, specimen and measurement method. The absolute CD4 count uses a count per volume, and INR and PT each need their corresponding lab limit. A potassium, magnesium or calcium measurement and its documented CTCAE v6.0 grade are distinct observations. CTCAE calcium grading can require a corrected or ionized value and clinical findings. The protocol’s pediatric formula is the 0.413 bedside Schwartz equation. A generic Schwartz label does not establish which equation the lab used. [LOINC helper T-cell count](https://loinc.org/24467-3/) · [Cockcroft–Gault clearance](https://loinc.org/35591-7/) · [NCI CTCAE v6.0](https://dctd.cancer.gov/research/ctep-trials/for-sites/adverse-events)

For cardiac and ophthalmic assessments the catalog records method and result separately. A MUGA study must supply a linked LVEF reading. A 12-lead ECG must supply a Fridericia-corrected QT result. A Snellen assessment must be documented as performed. [MUGA study](https://loinc.org/81567-0/) · [QTcF](https://loinc.org/76634-5/) · [Snellen](https://loinc.org/LA25498-9/)

`sotio_inhibitors.json` mirrors both tables in protocol 106S01 Amendment 1 §12.8, pages 100–101. It contains 20 strong CYP3A4 inhibitor entries and 19 P-gp inhibitor entries. Exact RxNorm ingredient lookup and returning concept properties verified all 36 unique ingredient identities against the November 2, 2026 release. The groups preserve complete combinations, including the two acceptable companion choices for paritaprevir plus ritonavir. Ritonavir also has its own independent CYP3A4 entry. Ingredients from unrelated events require an explicit coadministration link before they can satisfy one combination. Product identity, actual administration or stop, and the applicable elimination half-life each require evidence. [NLM RxNorm concept properties](https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/RxNav/APIs/api-RxNorm.getRxConceptProperties.html)

Tumor classification uses NCIt C9145 for osteosarcoma and C9306 for soft tissue sarcoma as candidate class roots. A specific histological subtype requires a validated mapping tied to the pathology diagnosis. Metastatic disease or advanced unresectability requires a separate assessment on the same tumor. [NCI Osteosarcoma](https://evsexplore.semantics.cancer.gov/evsexplore/concept/ncit/C9145) · [NCI Soft Tissue Sarcoma](https://evsexplore.semantics.cancer.gov/evsexplore/concept/ncit/C9306)

## Michigan

The Michigan protocol has three curated source bullets. For the diagnosis rule, the specific leiomyosarcoma code or histology and the assessment of disease extent must reference the same cancer. NCIt C14151 (unresectable) and C14174 (metastatic) are potential standardized assessment values on the indexed cancer; the existing synthetic extent observations used local question codes and Boolean values. [NCI Unresectable](https://api-evsrest.nci.nih.gov/api/v1/concept/ncit/C14151?include=summary) · [NCI Metastatic](https://api-evsrest.nci.nih.gov/api/v1/concept/ncit/C14174?include=summary) For the imaging rule, DICOM 126081 identifies the RECIST 1.1 method. A dated assessment, explicit measurability finding and governing baseline milestone are additional inputs. [NLM leiomyosarcoma concepts](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/medgen/9711) · [DICOM controlled terminology](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/output/chtml/part16/chapter_D.html)

The NLM property responses confirm doxorubicin 3639, gemcitabine 12574 and docetaxel 72962 as ingredients. Codes 1191138 and 1719003 identify specific clinical drugs. Code 376888 denotes a docetaxel injectable-solution clinical drug form (SCDF); it does not specify strength. Code 1790128 identifies a Doxil branded liposomal drug. First-line advanced-disease setting, same course, actual administration and the protocol policy for liposomal or planned therapy remain distinct. [Doxorubicin](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/1191138/properties.json) · [Gemcitabine](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/1719003/properties.json) · [Docetaxel drug form](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/376888/properties.json) · [Doxil](https://rxnav.nlm.nih.gov/REST/rxcui/1790128/properties.json)

## Local facts and authority

- The patient, tumor episode, study, site, cycle and dated consent are identifiers and relationships. Bind actual FHIR references, events and their provenance.
- Site cohort openings, regional authorization, sponsor agreement, exceptional access, investigator judgments and patient agreements are sourced decisions. Retain the versioned study-specific question or operation, who answered, the answer and its effective date. A disease or procedure code alone cannot supply these facts.
- Exposure confirmation, reviewed history completeness, a negative history, chronology and the reason for treatment require explicit evidence. A zero-result retrieval cannot establish a negative history.
- A partial coding candidate is labeled by its component. NYHA class does not cover the other cardiac diagnoses. The Gilbert code identifies one exception. A live MMR code is one positive example in a larger vaccine catalog. Clinical or source ambiguities remain with their recorded owner.
