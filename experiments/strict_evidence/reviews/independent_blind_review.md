The verifier read the original note and criterion for every non-training assignment, 134 pairs from 44 distinct notes. The publisher projection contained the annotation ID, patient ID, trial ID, criterion type, criterion text and note. The released strict predictions supplied the existing state. Physician annotations were unavailable to the review. The release baseline reproduced all 134 strict states, study IDs and note fingerprints.

Case 817 has a potentially decisive fact. The note identifies the episode as a hospitalization for cryptogenic stroke two weeks earlier. The next sentence explicitly places a brain CT and treatment in that episode. A diagnosed stroke is a vascular event even if its cause is unknown. A negative hemorrhage CT, thrombolysis, historical stroke unrelated to the current symptoms, or a stroke mimic cannot support this conclusion by themselves. In NCT01550588 the nonvascular-origin exclusion and the separate radiologically verified cryptogenic-stroke inclusion are different criteria. The latter would need separate evidence. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01550588) · [American Stroke Association terminology](https://www.stroke.org/en/stroke-facts)
